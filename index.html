Explore Butwal & Rupandehi.

Belbariya, Butwal-14 is nestled in the fertile Terai landscape of Rupandehi district, strategically located close to rich historical landmarks, cultural treasures, and the sacred garden of Lumbini.

Make D & D BBQ Zone your base for delicious dining and peaceful rest before exploring the best sights of the region:

Lumbini — Sacred Birthplace of Lord Buddha

A UNESCO World Heritage sanctuary only about 35 minutes away, featuring ancient monasteries, Maya Devi Temple, and peace gardens.

Jitgadhi Killa (Fortress)

The historic fortress along the Tinau River where brave Nepali forces defeated the British East India Company troops in 1815.

Hill Park & Manimukunda Sen Park

Enjoy panoramic views of Butwal city and the Chure mountain ridge, or stroll through the royal historic ruins of Phulbari park.
